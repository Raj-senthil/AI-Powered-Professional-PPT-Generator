# AI-Powered-Professional-PPT-Generator
AI-powered presentation generator that transforms simple ideas into stunning, professional PowerPoint presentations.

Simply enter a topic, choose your preferred theme and colour, add optional subheadings or custom instructions, and let AI do the rest.  **Key Features** - AI-powered PPT generation. - Custom themes and colour palettes. - Optional subheadings and design instructions. - Professional layouts, visuals and animations. - Individual slide editing and regeneration. - Export to PPTX and PDF.  **Goal:** Make professional presentation creation effortless, creative, and accessible to everyone.
